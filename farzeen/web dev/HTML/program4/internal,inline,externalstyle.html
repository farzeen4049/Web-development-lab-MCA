In Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET), Muvattupuzha

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET), Muvattupuzha, is a two-year professional post-graduate program designed to prepare students for core roles in the global software industry. Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and approved by the AICTE, the program admits 60 students annually, distributed equally between merit and management quotas. The curriculum blends advanced computer science theory with deep practical training, supported by specialized infrastructure like a dedicated department library and modern computing labs. Backed by a proactive Training and Placement Cell, the program consistently prepares graduates for successful careers as Software Developers and Systems Analysts, with alumni regularly secured by prominent tech firms like Wipro, IBS Software, and Speridian Technologies.

An MCA (Master of Computer Applications) student's dream is typically to transition from academic coding to a high-impact tech career—landing a role as a Software Developer, Full-Stack Engineer, Data Scientist, or Cloud/AI Specialist at a top product or service company.
